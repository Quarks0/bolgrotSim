Bolgrot's Spirit Simulator

About

The goal of this tool is to help players familiarize with the abilities and mechanics of the Fire Extinction encounter without the energy and death walk downtime. It also provides a failure reason, which was one of the biggest hurdles when personally learning the encounter.

The scenarios include a variety of enemy counts and semi-random starting positions. It's worth re-attempting the same arrangement a few times to familiarize with the fight mechanics.

Note: This is a non-commercial fan project that explores the mechanics of the Bolgrot's Spirit encounter as part of the Turquoise Dofus quest line.
